Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your membership with 30 days notice. No hidden fees.

Is there an enrollment fee?

No enrollment fee for annual memberships. Monthly memberships have a $50 one-time fee.

Can I bring a friend?

Premium members get 4 guest passes per month. Other members can purchase day passes for $15.

What amenities are included?

All members get access to locker rooms, showers, and free parking.
